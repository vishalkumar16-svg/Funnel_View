    Category rules (edit and apply)

    One line per rule: Category | Sub-disposition | Status | Stage. A lead’s calling status (inbound, outbound, PS-IB) is matched to the sub-disposition first, then its lead category fields, then Stage and Status. Enrolled is also detected from the enrollment fields. Edit or add lines, then apply.
